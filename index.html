FAQ

Common questions about Chinese wine labels

Do I need a Chinese label to sell wine in China? +
Yes. Under China's Food Safety Law, all imported prepackaged food — including wine — must carry a Chinese label before it can clear customs. The label must comply with GB 7718 (General Standard for the Labeling of Prepackaged Foods) and GB 2758 (fermented alcoholic beverages).
What must be shown on a Chinese wine label? +
Mandatory items include: product name, ingredient list (grape juice and sulfur dioxide), alcohol content (%vol), country of origin, net content (e.g. 750ml), importer's name, address and contact, production date, storage conditions, the warning "Excessive drinking is harmful to health", and the winery's China registration number.
How much can I save by sourcing wine labels from China? +
Typically 15% or more versus existing suppliers, depending on volume and finish. LabelBridge benchmarks your current labels and sources the same or better quality at lower cost.
How does the China–Georgia Free Trade Agreement affect wine labels? +
The China–Georgia FTA (effective January 2018) sets zero import duty on Georgian wine. It does not remove labeling requirements — every bottle still needs a GB-compliant Chinese label, which LabelBridge produces and delivers.
How does LabelBridge guarantee compliance? +
With over 10 years of adhesive label material know-how and China-side expertise in GB 7718, GB 2758 and GACC winery registration, LabelBridge handles design, printing and QC so every label clears customs.
